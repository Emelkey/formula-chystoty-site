# Link-building plan: Formula Chistoty

Updated: 2026-08-16

This is a manual outreach plan. No backlink, citation, partnership, review, or directory placement is counted as acquired until its live URL and publication date are recorded.

## Rules

- Use the canonical site URL: `https://www.formula-chistoty.ck.ua/`.
- Keep the public name, phone, hours, website, and service area consistent with the verified Google Business Profile.
- Do not create duplicate business profiles.
- Do not buy PBN, forum, comment, sitewide, or bulk-directory links.
- Prefer brand, URL, and natural descriptive anchors. Do not repeat exact-match commercial anchors at scale.
- A link must be useful to a local customer or genuinely relevant to cleaning, property, construction, furniture, automotive, or business operations.

## 90-day workflow

### Days 1-14

1. Audit every existing profile for name, phone, website, hours, and location/service-area consistency.
2. Resolve Top20, Misto.biz.ua, Cherkasy.biz, Facebook, and Instagram records before adding new profiles.
3. Create an outreach tracker with owner, contact, date, status, live URL, and next follow-up.

### Days 15-30

1. Contact five qualified local partners with one relevant asset each.
2. Ask two real B2B clients for permission to publish a short factual case or supplier mention.
3. Publish fresh real work photos to Google Business Profile weekly.

### Days 31-60

1. Secure one or two editorially reviewed local or partner mentions.
2. Produce one downloadable checklist only if it can be based on verified working practice.
3. Review link quality and referral traffic; do not optimize for raw link count.

### Days 61-90

1. Repeat the partnerships that produced relevant visits or leads.
2. Compare new referring domains with the previous period using an approved SEO tool.
3. Recheck Cleaning Services backlink gaps only when tool data is available; do not infer them from search results.

## Outreach tracker

Use the following columns:

`domain | page/contact | category | relevance | proposed asset | target URL | proposed anchor | first contact | follow-up | status | live URL | verified date | notes`

Allowed statuses: `TO RESEARCH`, `QUALIFIED`, `CONTACTED`, `FOLLOW-UP`, `APPROVED`, `LIVE`, `DECLINED`, `NO SAFE METHOD`.

## Success criteria

- More relevant referring domains, not more links at any cost.
- Consistent branded citations on authoritative local sources.
- Referral visits and qualified leads from partner pages.
- No manual-action risk, exact-match anchor pattern, or duplicate business profiles.
